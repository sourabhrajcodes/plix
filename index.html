FAQ

Quick answers

What footage should I send?

Phone walkthrough (60–120s, slow pan, lights on), plus any balcony/drone. Drive link works. Share your listing URL so I match the copy.

Do you edit drone?

Yes — DJI, Insta360, phone drone apps. I color-match drone to interior so exterior→interior feels like one property, not two grades.

Turnaround & revisions?

First cut in 48h (Pro: 36h). One revision round included — timing, text, music swap. Rush 24h available.

What do I get?

MP4 H.264 1080p: 16:9 (portals) + 9:16 (Reels/TikTok) + separate thumbnail frame + SRT captions on request.

Can you do virtual staging or 3D?

I do light virtual staging teasers and map animations. Full 3D renders — I partner with your 3D artist and edit the story.

How do I replace videos after?

Open js/projects.js, replace embedUrl with your YouTube or Drive preview link, and swap the SVG in assets/thumbs/. No code needed.
